# **Descent into the Utterdark Rules**

*These rules are still a WIP, and might change during the game. Please message a DM if you spot issues, typos, imbalances or want clarification on how the rules function.*

The megagame is made up of rounds, and within each game round each team takes a turn. Turns are submitted through a Team Tracker Sheet which you will have pinned in your Team Chat on Discord.

A game round usually spans a week in real life. During this time you can roleplay with other teams you have met and submit your actions. By the end of the week the DMs will resolve the actions of all of the teams, and the next round will begin at the start of the next week.

The main goal of the game is to expand by building outposts in unclaimed lands, support your people who have survived the collapse, and protect them from the monsters that lurk in the dark. Each team has their own goals as well, which could be anything from surviving until the “end of the game”, to annexing the lands of other teams, to collecting a menagerie full of exotic monsters.

You are entering a dangerous and mysterious new world. It may be your goal to explore, searching the ruins and extracting the unique resources of this land, and maybe learning the lore of this place delving into the dangerous Dungeons that you might find.

In order to support your people you will need to build Farms and Mines to secure a stockpile of Food and Gold. Miner Units will collect resources from the earth while combat units such as Regiments, Mages, Scouts, and Healers are able to protect your people and harvest resources from slain monsters, while Guards protect your important locations from hidden foes.

# **Using the Tracker**

Each team has access to a Team Tracker on *Google Sheets*. Within this tracker is a Reference Sheet for quick access to the rules, and a number of other sheets.

## **TURNS**

This is the most important sheet. Here you submit your 6 actions per turn.  
For each action:

- Set the Turn Number to the current turn  
- Set your Action Number for each Action. Actions, Conditional Actions, and Free Actions are resolved in the order that they are displayed in the sheet, top to bottom.  
- Write a description for your Action. Indicate Region Numbers (e.g. Move Regiment 1 from A1 to A3.) so that the DMs can efficiently resolve your actions. Write as much as you want. Include flavour. It will all be taken into account in how the action is resolved.

Once turns are resolved the DMs will fill in the Action Outcome and Resource Gain/Loss columns for you. Action Outcomes may be as simple as Building Constructed or Unit Recruited, or a detailed description of what your spies have witnessed or a play-by-play account of a battle.

At the top of the Turns Sheet is a brief overview of your current Gold reserve, Food reserve, and Growth. Use these to ensure that you don’t overspend during your turn. For more in depth information see the Tracker and Economy Sheets.

## **UNITS**

Here you will find all of your Units. This sheet is used both for keeping track of your units, and for the DMs to resolve combats quickly. (thus the Turn column)

The DMs will add and remove Units for you. All that you use this sheet for is monitoring your Unit’s health, and adding a Unit Name if you wish.

Units stats can be modified by the presence of Fortifications in a region, the Race of the unit, whether the unit was recruited in a region with an Academy, and the augments that they are equipped with.

## **REGIONS**

This sheet tracks your claimed regions, as well as the buildings within your regions. The DMs will modify this sheet during turn resolution to add regions, settlements, and buildings as they are constructed.

## **INVENTORY**

This sheet keeps a record of all of your items. It is modified by the DMs as you gain and lose items.

The Crafted Into column is important for the player. If an item is craftable, there will be a list of items that use the item in their crafting recipe. Looking at this column you can decide on items to Research using a Smithy.

## **CRAFTING RESEARCH**

This sheet contains a list of basic augments that you can craft using a Smithy. If you research new items using a Smithy, they will be added to the bottom of the list. Then you will be able to see what additional resources you will need to craft them in the Crafted From column.

## **TRACKER**

This sheet keeps track of all of your units, buildings, and settlements for each turn.

## **ECONOMY**

This sheet uses the Tracker Sheet to calculate your Cash Flow across turns. The more economical minded player may be interested in tracking their projected profit and loss of Gold, Food, and Growth.

## **REFERENCE**

This sheet provides a rundown of all the rules you should need to play the game\!

With all of these sheets at your disposal you can efficiently submit your actions, and the DMs can resolve your turns, hopefully allowing you more time to focus on roleplaying your interactions with other teams\!

# **Actions**

Each round you have 6 Actions. You do not have to use all of these actions, but there should be plenty for you to interact with.

The order in which you submit actions matters. Action 1 will be resolved first by the DMs alongside Action 1 of all the other players.

**Free Actions** do not count towards your 6 Actions, but they are limited by requirements. You are limited to one Free Action per unit or building per turn if the Free Action requires a unit or building.

Some actions appear in both the Actions and Free Actions list. If you use a Free Action, such as the miner’s Excavate Free Action, you can then use an Action to Excavate again, but only once per turn.

**Conditional Actions** can be used up to *Two Times* per turn. These actions only occur if the original action cannot. In your Turns sheet, the Conditional Action should be placed *after* the original Action.

## **Actions List:**

**Annex Region**

Claim a region already claimed by another team. The region must not contain any enemy units, and must contain one of your own **Regiment** units.

**Attack**

Attack a unit in the same region as you, either from stealth, to initiate combat with a neutral/friendly unit, or to fight a monster that you have found within a region that has yet to attack you. Can be used to get stealthed scouts to join a combat when other units enter a region to support. If you successfully surprise the units being attacked (i.e. from stealth) you gain advantage on initiative and your first attack in combat.

***NOTE**: You only need to use the attack action when combat would not usually automatically occur. When a unit moves into another region containing a hostile unit, combat begins unless the description of the Move action states that your unit would retreat or act peacefully.*

**Construct Building**

You pay for and construct a building in a region you own which has an available building slot.

**Construct Outpost**

You pay for and construct an outpost in a region not owned by yourself or another faction, and has no hostile units within it. Construction costs 3 less if a Ruin is present.

**Demolish**

Specify a building you wish to demolish and the region it is constructed. You will demolish the building and salvage half of the initial construction cost. You will not pay the building upkeep on the turn it was demolished.

**Excavate** *(1 per Miner Unit)*

Excavate a tunnel in a specified direction. Units will be able to move through the tunnel if it opens up access to a new region. You may also uncover resources or dangers. You must have a miner in the region you plan to excavate, and raw resources claimed through mining produce 2 income.  
The Miner unit uses 1 movement during this action as it moves into the newly excavated passage or adjoining region. Thus, your Miner must have at least 1 movement remaining to be able to use this action.

***NOTE**: Excavate is both an Action and a Free Action. Each unit can extract one per turn for free, and you can use an Action to excavate an additional time if you wish.*

**Extract Deposit** *(Requires Miner Unit)*

You attempt to extract resources from a known deposit. The specified unit(s) within the region will make either a Strength or Constitution check depending on the task, which will determine the success of the action.

**Extract Monster**

You attempt to extract resources from a dead Monster Unit. The specified unit(s) within the region  will make a Dexterity check or Wisdom check depending on the task, which will determine the success of the action. 

***NOTE**: Extract Resource is both an Action and a Free Action. Each unit can extract one per turn for free, and you can use an Action to extract an additional time if you wish.*

**Heal Unit**

Heal a unit to a specified health. Costs 1 income per 5 hit points recovered (rounded up, minimum cost of 1 income) and 1 food per 10 hit points. You can mix and match Gold and Food. A unit healed in this way must be in a region you control.  
If you have a Healer present you can heal one unit to full HP at no cost per Healer. A unit healed in this way does not need to be in a region you control.

**Improvise**

Describe something that you want to do that is not otherwise described by an existing Action.

***NOTE**: You can put gold, food or even resources towards an improvised action in order to improve the probability of a positive outcome \- such as putting gold and food towards hosting a banquette for diplomats*

**Move Unit**

Choose one unit to move through a number of regions up to its movement. This movement provokes combat if it moves into a region containing enemy units, unless specified in the Action Description.

***NOTE:** The Movement speed of a unit is a limit per turn, not per action. If a unit has a movement speed of 2 it can only move two regions per turn, no matter how many actions you use.*

**Move Unit \- Stealth** *(Requires Scout or Assassin Unit)*

Choose one unit to move through a number of units up to half its speed. This unit makes a stealth check for each region moved through, and on a success avoids combat if it moves past or ends its turn in a region containing hostile units.

***NOTE**: The stealth DC is equal to the highest passive perception of all Units or Watchtowers within the region you are traversing. Passive perception equals 10 \+ Wisdom Modifier*

**Move Unit \- Force March**

Choose one unit to move through a number of regions up to twice its movement. This movement provokes combat if it moves into a region containing enemy units, unless specified in the Action Description. Units moved in this way have disadvantage on all attacks, ability checks, and saves made during the course of the movement.

***NOTE: COMBINING MOVEMENT***  
*When a unit moves through a region containing another unit controlled by you, you may choose to pick it up and move it alongside the original moving unit. The picked up unit cannot move exceeding its own movement or the movement of the original unit. You can choose to drop off any unit in a region you move through.*  
*When using Force March, picked up units can also move up to twice their movement, and have disadvantage on all attacks, ability checks, and saves made for the rest of the turn.*  
*When using Stealth, picked up units also move at half speed, and  you may only pick up other Scouts of Assassins.*

**Raid**

Specify a region controlled by another faction. Specify a unit currently within that region, or that will move through that region within your turn. That unit steals an amount of gold and food equal to half of what that region produces. If your unit dies in battle within the specified region you gain no gold or food.

**Reanimate** *(Requires Necromancer)*

When this unit is in a region containing a dead humanoid unit they may raise that unit as a Zombie unit. Alternatively, expend one Pristine Bones item to raise a Skeleton Unit.

*(NOTE: Zombie and Skeleton Units require no upkeep. Other units such as Wraiths and Flesh Golems could be created with this action in exchange for more items or sacrifices)*

**Recruit Unit**

Pay for and recruit a specified unit within a region you control.

**Search**

Specify whether you want to search for resources in a region,  hidden units, or to search a structure such as a Ruin or Dungeon, and which units you want to engage in the search. Units make a Wisdom, Intelligence, or Charisma check. Success may lead to discovering Deposits of resources, combat with hidden units, and you may encounter monsters and treasure within Ruins or Dungeons.  
   
**Spy** *(Requires Scout or Assassin Unit)*

You will receive insight on a region. You must have a stealthed unit within the region you plan to spy on. Your unit will make either a Wisdom, Intelligence or Charisma check to learn about the region (whichever is higher).

**Teleport** *(Requires Archmage)* 

The Archmage can attempt to teleport to a region that the wizard is familiar with. There is a 80% chance the Archmage will arrive at their destination, a 15% chance they will arrive 1 region away, 5% chance they’ll appear in a random region.

**Upgrade Building**

You pay for and upgrade a building that you have already constructed to its next tier.

**Use Portal** *(Requires a Portal)*

If you are in a region with a portal you may use this action to transport units through it. You must have a destination which you can access before using this action. This would either be another portal connected to the portal you are using, a Portal Building in another region that you control, or another faction’s portal you have been given access to.

## **Free Actions List:**

**Apply Augment** *(No limit)*

Applies an augment from your inventory to a unit.

**Capture Monster** *(1 per Unit)*	

Use this Action when you intend to enter a combat with a known Monster Unit. Specify the monster unit. In combat, once the specified unit is below one quarter hit points and there are no other enemy units in combat, each of your units will use one action to attempt to capture the unit instead of attacking. On a success the monster is captured.

A captured monster must be escorted to another region where it can be released or placed within a Breeding Pit or Menagerie before the end of the next Game Round.

**Craft/Research** *(1 per Smithy)*

Create an item from your research tab assuming you have the resources to do so OR research an item or material from your inventory which can be crafted into another item, as seen in the "Crafted Into" column on the "Inventory" tab. You research 1d4 items from the list. Please specify which items you would prioritise researching.

*NOTE: researching is the main way that you will discover how to craft other items. You may however trade knowledge with other players or find recipes in Ruins or Dungeons.*

**Disband Unit** *(No Limit)*

Disband one unit currently within a region that you control. All equipped augments are returned to your inventory.

**Divine Intervention** *(1 per Temple)*

When using this action, describe the assistance you seek, and from which God. Should your prayer be heard your deity will attempt to help you, be it through guidance or direct boons.

**Excavate** *(1 per Miner)*

Excavate a tunnel in a specified direction. Units will be able to move through the tunnel if it opens up access to a new region. You may also uncover resources or dangers. You must have a miner in the region you plan to excavate, and raw resources claimed through mining produce 2 income.

**Extract Deposit** *(Requires Miner Unit \- 1 per Miner Unit)*

You attempt to extract resources from a known deposit. The specified unit(s) within the region will make either a Strength or Constitution check depending on the task, which will determine the success of the action.

**Extract Monster** *(1 per Unit)*

You attempt to extract resources from a dead Monster Unit. The specified unit(s) within the region  will make a Dexterity check or Wisdom check depending on the task, which will determine the success of the action. 

***NOTE**: Extract Resource is both an Action and a Free Action. Each unit can extract one per turn for free, and you can use an Action to extract an additional time if you wish.*

**Spy** *(1 per Scout or Assassin)*

You receive insight on the activities within a specified region. You must have a stealthed unit within the region you plan to spy on.

**Store Beast** *(1 per Beastmaster)*

Store a tamed beast into a Menagerie. Your Beastmaster must be in the same region as the Menagerie.

**Swap/Retrieve Beast** *(1 per Beastmaster)*

Swap a tamed beast with a beast currently stored in a Menagerie. Your Beastmaster must be in the same region as the Menagerie.

**Tame Beast** *(1 per Beastmaster)*

Use this Free Action when you move a Beastmaster into a region containing a Monster Unit that you wish to tame. The Beastmaster attempts to tame the beast before combat begins. On a success the Beast Master must spend the rest of the turn in the region with the monster. On a fail the Beast may retreat, or attack on a bad fail.

**Trade** *(No limit)*

Trade of resources between players. Please specify what you are sending and receiving and from who. You must have made contact with the player in the game to trade with them. You may also trade other things such as your Research (augment recipes), map information, units, or even regions.

**Use Market** *(Requires Market, No limit)*

Allows you to trade food for income or income for food if you have access to a Market Building. You can exchange 4 gold for 1 food, or 1 food for 2 gold.

**Use Resource** *(No limit)*

Allows you to items which are used outside of combat, or to convert various items into materials or income.

# **Items and Augments**

During your adventures in the Utterdark, you will come across useful items that can be equipped by your units. Some items you can craft at a smithy, whereas others you may find in the world or trade with other players.

Equippable items are known as Augments. It is a Free Action to equip a Unit with an Augment.  
There are restrictions regarding how many Augments can be equipped on one Unit.  
A Unit can be equipped with no more than **three** augments, and these may be no more than:

- One Armour Augment.  
- One Consumable Augment.  
- One Equipment Augment.  
- One Shield Augment.  
- One Weapon or Tool Augment.

Non-equippable items include:

- Basic Material: These are resources gathered from your surroundings such as Metal, Wood, and Gemstones. They are used to craft Augments.  
- Item: These are special objects which convey unique abilities.  
- Material: These are usually resources gathered from defeated monsters. They can be used to craft Augments.  
- Trinket: These are descriptive items to convey lore, or to be collected.

# **Regions**

The Map is composed of huge regions. Regions will contain monsters, resources, and structures.

Unclaimed regions can be claimed using the *Construct Outpost* action so long as there are no overt dangers within the region. Claiming a region secures an area for your people to live, work and build.

Regions claimed by another faction can be claimed using the *Annex* action, which requires that they region is free of enemy units and you have a *Regiment Unit* within the region.

When a region is claimed you might think it safe, but this is not the case. Each region is vast, and your people are few. There will surely be hidden things, and until you have thoroughly searched a region using the *Search Action* you cannot be certain that monsters dwelling in caves won't jump out to attack later on.

Regions can contain structures, such as ruins of old settlements or dungeons. These can be explored using the *Search Action*, and used to your benefit if they are not too dangerous.

# **Settlements**

Your faction requires settlements to house its people. When you have a settlement your people can construct buildings and you can begin recruiting units.

Each region can support one settlement. This settlement can be upgraded to allow for more building slots.

You can build new settlements or expand existing settlements by using growth, alongside any required gold or food cost.

Growth represents your faction's ability to grow, such as surplus supplies, building materials and population. Each settlement you have will generate growth, as well as infrastructure buildings such as farms.

**Outpost**

Can be constructed within an unclaimed region. Costs 5 Gold income to construct, or 2 Gold if constructed on an existing Ruin. Can support one building.

**Settlement**

Can be constructed in a region containing an Outpost. Costs 15 income to construct. Can support two buildings.

**City**

Can be constructed in a region containing a Settlement. Costs 25 income to construct. Can support three buildings.

**Capital**	

Can be constructed in a region containing a City. Costs 40 income to construct. Can support four buildings.You can only have one capital in your faction.

# **Economy**

When the DMs resolve your actions for a Game Round they will calculate your economy. At the start of your next turn you will have gained food, gold, and Growth from buildings. Trades between teams are resolved and upkeep costs for all of your units and buildings are paid.

It is your job to manage your economy, keeping your people fed and supplying them with the motivation (gold/currency) to work and expand.

If you have negative Food, Gold or Growth at the end of your turn some of your actions that lead to that may not resolve, or random events may occur to punish your reckless spending. Your units may disband and your people revolt.

GOLD: The universal currency. Gold does not represent actual gold, but a combination of wealth and your ability to motivate your people to carry out your vision.

FOOD: This value represents your stockpiles of food used to feed your workers and units.

GROWTH: Growth represents your faction's ability to grow, such as surplus supplies, building materials and population. Each settlement you have will generate growth, as well as infrastructure buildings such as farms.
